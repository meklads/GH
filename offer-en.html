Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

Do you work with developers outside Saudi Arabia?

add

Yes. We have active offices and project delivery experience in Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain, and Egypt، and we regularly deliver for GCC-based developers with international projects. Travel and logistics are fully managed by our team.

What is your typical project timeline?

add

It depends on scope. A cinematic CGI film typically takes 3–6 weeks from brief approval. A smart maquette with interactive systems ranges from 6–12 weeks. Exhibition and sales center fit-outs are structured around your launch or event date. We'll map a precise timeline during your initial consultation.

Can we commission individual services, or is it a bundled package?

add

All five services are available individually or in any combination. Many clients start with a single deliverable، a CGI film or smart maquette، and expand as the project progresses. We build a scope that fits your current phase and budget.

How do you handle revisions and feedback?

add

Each project has a dedicated Project Manager who is your single point of contact throughout. We build structured review milestones into every production، you approve before we proceed to the next stage. There are no surprises at delivery.

What information do you need to start?

add

For CGI and visualization: architectural drawings, material specs, and any reference imagery. For maquettes and interactive platforms: the same, plus a clear project brief. For exhibitions: your stand dimensions and event date. We guide you through what's needed in the first call، you don't need everything ready before reaching out.

What sets Graphics House apart from other CGI studios?

add

Most studios produce renders. We produce sales systems. The difference is strategy، every deliverable we produce is designed to serve a specific moment in your buyer's journey. We also offer the full spectrum in-house: CGI, smart maquettes, interactive software, exhibition design, and media production. No other studio in the GCC combines all five at this quality level.
