    Frequently asked questions

    What is the difference between a touchscreen and a full interactive experience?

    A screen only shows content. A full experience connects the screen to the maquette, the data, the visitor path and the sales team, so the gallery becomes a persuasion tool.

    Can you connect the experience to a maquette we already have?

    Yes. We connect screens and interfaces to an existing maquette or to a new one we build with you, as in the Makkah Charter project.

    Do you train the sales team to run it?

    Training is part of the interactive gallery level, and an acceptance rehearsal before launch day is part of the full system.

    How long does it take?

    It depends on the data complexity and the gallery opening date. We set the schedule after a short session where we review the project scope.

    Do you work outside Saudi Arabia?

    Yes. We have a presence in Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain and serve projects across the Gulf.
