Smart Maquette Interactive Systems Interior Design Makkah
The Challenge

Project Overview

The Muslim World League، one of the most prestigious institutions in the Islamic world، commissioned Graphics House to design and execute the complete visual identity and interactive experience for their global headquarters in Makkah. The deadline was fixed to the official inauguration date. There was no room for delay, and the standard of execution had to match the institution's standing.
