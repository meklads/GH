Frequently asked questions

Brief answers for potential partners.

Is Graphics House a competitor to my agency?

No. We are a specialist production partner — you keep the client and strategy; we execute the visual and experiential layer.

Do you offer white label?

Yes. Some projects can be structured as white label according to the partnership and agreement, with full confidentiality and protection of your client relationship. Co-Delivery and joint production models are also available.

Do you need us to hand over the client?

No. Your agency can retain the client relationship and account management; we define the working model per project.

Can we use Graphics House in pitches and pitch decks?

Yes. We can support proposals that require our specialist capabilities — including before the project is awarded.

How do we start?

With a 20–30 minute introduction, then scope definition, followed by joint execution with a clear framework.

How quickly do you respond?

Our partnerships team reviews every request and responds within 24 business hours.
