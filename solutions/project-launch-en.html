FAQ

What decision-makers ask before they book

What does ProjectLaunch™ include?

Visual identity, CGI renders and films, smart maquettes, interactive experiences, sales-gallery décor, photography and direction — one system.

Must every element ship at once?

Not necessarily. We help you prioritize by stage and budget. The real value shows when everything speaks one visual language.

We already have a marketing agency — do we still need ProjectLaunch™?

Usually yes. The agency moves the message. ProjectLaunch™ builds the launch product itself — the visual language, persuasion assets, and sales experience. Many campaigns underperform because the visual product is not ready.

Do you install on site?

Yes. Décor, screens, maquettes, and interactive systems are installed and handed over with team training.

Do you serve projects outside Riyadh?

Yes — across Saudi cities and the Gulf (UAE, Oman, Bahrain, Egypt), with production and on-site installation.
