Visual Launch Readiness Checklist

3dgraphicshouse.com

12 essentials to verify before announcing off-plan sales. Use with your marketing, sales, and visualization partners.

  1. 1Project positioning and target buyer persona defined
  2. 2Sales timeline aligned with visual production schedule
  3. 3Master plan and key views approved for visualization
  4. 4Brand guidelines or visual direction document ready
  5. 5Hero renders planned for launch campaign
  6. 6Sales gallery or digital platform requirements clear
  7. 7Video / CGI film scope defined (length, language, channels)
  8. 8Interactive experience scope defined (if applicable)
  9. 9Maquette or physical model decision made
  10. 10Content approval workflow with stakeholders
  11. 11Launch checklist shared with sales team
  12. 12Post-launch asset update plan in place
