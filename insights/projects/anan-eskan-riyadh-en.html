Anan Eskan: archviz and maquette for a Riyadh residential community

Photorealistic renders, CGI films, and architectural maquette for a major capital community launch, with documented exhibition and media assets.

Project: Residential Community, Riyadh

Exterior rendersCGI filmArchitectural maquette

Watch interview / clip

Anan Eskan is among the active developers in Riyadh's residential market. The project needed visuals clarifying density, green space, and unit variety before day one of sales, plus exhibition presence that speaks to institutional buyers, not a luxury villa template.

The challenge

Buyers ask about organization, massing, and amenities before façade beauty. Without a maquette and clear operational scenes, the booth becomes generic promises. The brief required a ProjectLaunch™ pack linking archviz, film, and maquette under one lighting language.

What Graphics House delivered

We delivered CGI and maquette assets serving both the media campaign and sales gallery with consistent lighting and angles across deliverables. The maquette clarifies massing for institutional visitors; renders support digital campaigns and brokers.

  • Photoreal exterior stills for launch and campaign
  • Short CGI film explaining community and amenities
  • Architectural maquette for gallery and exhibitions
  • Exhibition presence assets: poster and CEO media interview

Outcome and exhibition presence

Assets became ready for sales teams, brokers, and a media moment in a Restatex context. See the full report: Anan Eskan at Restatex & the Amlak Interview. For workforce housing market context see Makkah B2B workforce housing viz as a separate type reading from the Riyadh portfolio.

After assets lock, campaigns can amplify via Beesmotion. Supporting media via Dot4Life production after scene approval.

FAQ

What did the Anan Eskan delivery include?

Exterior renders, CGI film, architectural maquette, and exhibition assets documented in the Restatex report.

Why does the maquette matter in operational housing?

Institutional buyers read massing and capacity in seconds; the maquette shortens PDF explanation and proves seriousness at exhibitions.

Is there video for the project?

Yes, a CEO media interview in exhibition context, shown on the project page and report.
