Makkah Charter: smart maquette and interactive environment for the Muslim World League

Integrated décor, design, and execution for the Muslim World League: interactive maquette, control and display screens, and interactive programs, including the Jeddah Ramadan Forum delivery.

Project: Makkah Charter

Smart maquetteProjection mappingInteractive screensExhibition design

The Muslim World League is among the most prominent institutions in the Islamic world. The brief required exhibition experiences worthy of its initiatives and charters before international guests, in Makkah and Jeddah, without visual exaggeration or claims that cannot be defended before an institutional audience.

The challenge

The brief was not a traditional model, but an integrated visual-digital system explaining projects and initiatives in minutes. The window was tight, and acceptance criteria could not tolerate experimentation on opening day. The frame in Makkah visualization sensitivity applies to tone and angles.

What Graphics House delivered

Graphics House delivered décor, design, and full execution from one studio: a smart maquette of flagship League projects, interactive screens for detail and control, and interactive programs for media content and commemorative photos, with visual consistency from first touchpoint to final screen.

  • Interactive maquette at roughly 3×3 m linked to a large detail screen
  • Tablet for per-project lighting and display control
  • World press interactive program
  • Commemorative photo program inside the exhibition
  • Integrated décor and install under institutional events

Outcome: Jeddah Ramadan Forum

At the Jeddah Ramadan Forum the approach showed in practice: an interactive maquette as the central explanation point on the protocol path, with high-level acceptance of the shipped vision. See the full report: Ramadan Forum visual report. For residential Makkah context see Al Khair Heights as a separate portfolio case.

Supporting films via Dot4Life production after scene approval.
