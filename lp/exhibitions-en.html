The units

Four units that draw visitors in and can capture their data

01

Smart maquette

An architectural maquette of your projects with dynamic lighting. It draws visitors to the booth and shows the project before it is built.

02

Project viewer

A touch screen that drives the maquette and shows each project's details. It shortens persuasion: visitors see and choose for themselves.

03

News and achievements viewer

An interactive screen with the organisation's news, initiatives and achievements, browsed by the visitor.

04

Commemorative photo wall

A photo screen with custom exhibition frames; visitors take their photo and share it instantly. In your booth it can be linked to a QR code and consent form to capture visitor data.

The booth space and plan from the organiser, your identity, the projects or content to show, and the exhibition date.
