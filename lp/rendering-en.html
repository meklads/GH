FAQ

Before you start

What do you need to start?

Your latest drawings (CAD or PDF), material and colour references, the target audience, where the images will be published, and the launch date. Not every detail needs to be final.

I do not know how many images I need

We decide together in the session, based on where they will be published: ads, brochure, website, exhibition screen. A focused set of shots is worth more than a large set that goes unused.

Do the images match the drawings?

Yes. We build the model from approved drawings and add no upgrades the buyer will not receive.

How is the price set?

With a quote and written scope after we review the drawings, the number of shots and the deliverables, because scope differs from project to project.

What is the difference between a render and a film?

A render sells a single view. A film explains movement, location and facilities. You can start with renders and add the film later. Read the comparison

Do you handle projects near the Two Holy Mosques?

Yes. We have projects in Makkah and respect the sensitivity and character of these projects in our visuals.

Who holds the usage rights?

Usage rights are stated clearly in the quote before we start.
