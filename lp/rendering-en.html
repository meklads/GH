FAQ

Before you start

What do I need to start the renders?

Your latest drawings (CAD or PDF), material and colour references, the target audience, where the images will be shown, and the launch date. Not every detail needs to be final.

Do the images match the drawings?

Yes. We build the model from approved drawings and add no upgrades the buyer will not receive.

How is the price set?

With a quote after we review the drawings, the number of shots and the deliverables, because scope differs from project to project.

What is the difference between a render and a film?

A render sells a single view. A film explains movement, location and facilities. You can start with renders and add the film later. Read the comparison

Do you work in Riyadh, Jeddah and Makkah?

Yes. We are based in Saudi Arabia, have projects in these cities, and respect the sensitivity of projects near the Two Holy Mosques.

Do you provide materials in Arabic and English?

Yes, from one team and in one visual language.
