Our Process

Four Layers.
One Seamless Experience.

Each smart maquette is built in four stages، physical precision first, digital intelligence second.

01

Handcrafted Physical Model

Precision-built from premium materials at exact scale، villa, tower, masterplan, or city-scale installation. Every architectural detail is reproduced.

02

Digital Intelligence Layer

Projection mapping systems, embedded sensors, and programmable LED zones are integrated into the physical model، invisible until activated.

03

Interactive Control Software

Custom software lets your team highlight zones, toggle unit availability, trigger lighting sequences, and run presentations from a tablet.

04

Live Data Integration

Connect the maquette to your sales CRM or data feed، so the model reflects real-time unit status and project progress automatically.
