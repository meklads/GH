The Challenge

Project Overview

Al Rajhi، one of Saudi Arabia's most recognized names in real estate، required a complete visual sales system for their residential development. The project needed to perform across multiple touchpoints: the sales center showroom, real estate exhibitions, and investor presentations. A single deliverable wouldn't be sufficient، the visual experience had to be cohesive across every channel.
