Our Story

From a Jeddah studio
to a Gulf launch partner.

Graphics House was founded in Jeddah on a simple belief: developments deserve a visual language as ambitious as their architecture. We started in architectural visualization and grew into an integrated visual launch studio.

Today we work from offices in Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain, and Egypt, with field teams serving developers on residential, commercial, and institutional projects across the region.

We bring cinematic CGI, smart maquettes, interactive platforms, and spatial experiences into one system. Not separate deliverables from different vendors, but sales-ready tools built by one team.
