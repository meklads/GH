Project path

A package for every stage of your project

Do not pay for what you do not need now. Each package is built for a specific stage of the project life cycle. When your project advances, you move to the next one.

Choose your project's stage to see the right package.

Pre-launch

Basic Package

Early marketing tools for the project: teaser film, concept maquette, and identity, before launch

For: A project in study, design, or awaiting approval.

Duration: Set in the quote | Revision rounds: 1

Includes

  • Short cinematic 3D teaser film for the project
  • Concept architectural maquette to present massing and site
  • Project visual identity: logo, colors, and delivery files
  • 12 preliminary architectural images (CGI Concept): façades, exteriors, limited interiors
  • Landing page introducing the project with an interest form
  • Introductory brochure, print and digital
  • Investor and partner presentation (Project Deck)
What we need from you

Preliminary plans, proposed materials and colors, the project name and location.

Most requested During construction

Early Sales Package

Early-sales engine: cinematic 3D film, smart maquette, and interactive experience, during construction

For: A project under construction that is starting to market its units now.

Duration: Set in the quote | Revision rounds: 2

Includes

  • Cinematic 3D film for the project (4 to 5 minutes)
  • Smart architectural sales maquette (dynamic lighting by scope)
  • Interactive experience: touchscreen and/or 360° tour for units and availability
  • 18 final architectural images (CGI): exterior and interior, day and night
  • 3D floor plans for the units
  • Multi-page project website
  • Digital sales catalogue + social campaign cuts in platform sizes
  • Drone footage of the site (~60 seconds) and project editorial copy
What we need from you

Approved unit plans, a preliminary list of features and prices, and existing site photos if any.

After completion

Full Launch Package

Full spatial launch: sales gallery and booth wrapping film, maquette, and interactive with print and campaigns

For: A completed project or one close to handover.

Duration: Set in the quote | Revision rounds: 2

Includes

  • Sales gallery décor: design and build
  • Exhibition booth: design and build
  • Integration of the architectural maquette and interactive displays into the gallery and booth
  • Printed and digital catalogue, brochure, and unit specification sheets
  • Site hoarding and signage
  • Launch advertising campaign: cuts and ads for platforms
  • Landing page / digital update for remaining-unit launch
  • One day of on-site photography covering completion and handover
What we need from you

List of available units and prices, the launch date, and access to the site or gallery.
